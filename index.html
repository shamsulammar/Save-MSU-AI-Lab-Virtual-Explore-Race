How this works

This is a self-contained browser game. Each team opens this same link on one device (phone or laptop), enters their team name, and races through 7 stations to stop the A.I.R.E.S. virus before the 60‑minute clock runs out.

01

Sequential stations

Stations unlock one at a time. Solve a station to earn a Security Key and open the next.

02

Scoring

+100 points per correct solve. −20 points per wrong attempt. Score can't go below 0.

03

Timer

60:00 countdown starts when the mission begins and never pauses or resets.

04

Hints

3 hints total for the whole race. Each hint costs 10 points.

05

Finish

Collect all 7 keys, enter the final override code at the Server Room, and the virus is deactivated.

06

Progress saves

If a browser tab closes by accident, reopening the link resumes that team's run.

Facilitator note: each team's score lives in that device's browser only — there's no shared server. Have teams screenshot their Victory screen (or read out their score) so you can rank teams at the end.
